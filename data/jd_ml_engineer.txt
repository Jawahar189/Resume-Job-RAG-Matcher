Machine Learning Engineer - GenAI Platform

About the role
We are building an LLM-powered assistant for our enterprise customers. You will design, build and operate
retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems in production.

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience as an ML engineer or data scientist shipping models to production
- Strong Python and software engineering fundamentals, including unit testing
- Hands-on experience with LLMs and prompt engineering
- Experience building RAG pipelines with vector databases such as Pinecone, Weaviate or pgvector
- Experience deploying services with Docker and Kubernetes on AWS or GCP
- Familiarity with MLOps practices: model monitoring, evaluation and CI/CD
- Ability to communicate technical trade-offs clearly to non-technical stakeholders

Nice to have
- Experience with LangChain or LlamaIndex
- Knowledge of PostgreSQL and Redis
- Contributions to open-source ML projects

What we offer
- Hybrid work, learning budget, health insurance
